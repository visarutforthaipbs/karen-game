Caption lines are editable trailer text, not a new brand slogan. No fake countdown, added surveillance UI or concept scenes presented as gameplay. Game audio must be cleared and voice/music balanced; do not drown urgent gameplay sounds with trailer music. Capture 1920×1080 or higher with readable HUD. Check frame rate, subtitle safe areas, music rights and the final encode by watching/listening from beginning to end.

## Three short clip briefs

1. **Before the scan:** 12 seconds showing actual hot embers, player spraying and the thermal outcome. Caption: “The fire is out. Is the heat gone?” / “ไฟดับแล้ว แต่ความร้อนหมดหรือยัง?”
2. **Mutual aid:** 12 seconds showing companion spraying/raking while the player handles another spot. Caption describes the concrete action; no multiplayer claim.
3. **The next harvest:** 15 seconds connecting burn result with rice reserves and next-year preparation. Demonstrate that success has a village consequence.

Vertical capture should reframe the camera/recording for legibility, not crop away the game's essential controls. Each published campaign gets source/medium/campaign/content parameters. Prepare the URL only after the base App ID exists.
