## About this game — English

Under Two Skies is a single-player strategy and survival simulation about rotational upland farming under surveillance.

Your workday runs from 14:00 to 20:00. Prepare the plot, read the wind and work alongside your crew. Fire can prepare the next harvest, but letting it spread—or leaving hot embers behind—can put the village at risk.

**Prepare, burn, cool.** Build firebreaks, choose when to light the plot and use water where it matters. A successful burn is only part of the job: the remaining heat must cool before the night satellite pass.

**Work together.** Your companions help with the work. Coordinate their actions and respond when smoke, fire and surveillance disrupt the plan.

**Keep the village fed.** Return to the village between plots. Manage rice reserves and prepare for the next season as wind, drought and surveillance change the conditions of your campaign.

The game uses stylized, faceted landscapes and a simplified model of farming, fire and satellite detection. It is an interpretation of these relationships, rather than a field guide or a factual reconstruction of a specific event.
